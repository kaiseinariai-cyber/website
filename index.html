How ARC began

Born from one athlete's injury and a team's wake-up call.

Our Story

When our star player tore his ACL, it hit our whole team hard. Being sidelined for months meant watching from the outside as everyone else moved forward. The self-doubt and feeling of being left behind almost made him walk away from the sport completely.

Realizing how lonely injury recovery can be, and how few resources exist for student-athletes going through it, we created Athlete Reset Collective to help athletes reset, rebuild, and come back stronger.

Mission

To equip, support, and inspire recovering student-athletes by delivering curated physical tools, mental health guidance, and community resources directly to those navigating injury recovery.

Vision

A world where every student-athlete has the holistic care and community strength needed to overcome physical setbacks, rebuild their mindset, and thrive on and off the field.
