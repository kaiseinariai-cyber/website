What's in the ARC Box

Each ARC Recovery Box is built to help student-athletes care for their body and mindset while they work through injury recovery.

Direct impact

Every ARC Recovery Box Costs Just $15

Each $15 box equips a local student-athlete with physical recovery tools and mental health resources designed to help them navigate the challenges of injury recovery.

Athlete Reset Collective is a registered 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit organization, and contributions are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law.
